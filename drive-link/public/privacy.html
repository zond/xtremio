xtremio — privacy

Last updated 25 September 2026

xtremio is a media player. This page describes the one part of it that touches a Google Account: connecting a television to files in your Google Drive by scanning a code with your phone.

What it asks Google for

One permission, drive.file. In Google's words, it lets an app "see, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app". It cannot list your Drive, search it, or read anything you have not handed over. The file chooser you see is Google's own, runs on Google's servers, and tells this service nothing except which files you picked — you may pick one or several, and it is told about those and no others.

What is stored, and for how long

  • While you are pairing — a short-lived record holding the access and refresh tokens Google issued, the id, name and type of each file you chose, and whether the device that started the pairing asked to be reopened afterwards. It is deleted the moment your television collects it, and in any case automatically after ten minutes.
  • After pairing — nothing. This service keeps no account, no history of what you played, and no copy of your tokens.
  • A counter — to stop the service being used to run up a bill, it counts requests per hour against a one-way hash, never against you or your account. It expires within two hours.

What your television keeps

The refresh token goes to your television and stays there, so it can ask for a fresh access token while a long film is playing. It is stored by the app on that device. Nothing else is kept.

Who else sees it

Nobody. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third party of any kind. The only servers involved are Google's and this one, which runs on Google Cloud. Your files' contents are read by your television directly from Google Drive and never pass through this service.

Taking it back

Revoke this app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. That invalidates the token on your television immediately; the next thing it tries to play will stop and ask you to pair again. To remove what is on the television, clear the app's data or uninstall it.

This is a personal project

xtremio is free software, written in the open. What this page describes is what the code does, and the code is at github.com/zond/xtremio, where anything here can be checked rather than taken on trust. Questions and complaints belong in that project's issues.
